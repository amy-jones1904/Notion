I. My Tale

Greetings, fellow traveller. I am Amy — a humble hobbit with an unusually adventurous streak. My journey into the craft of code began in the year 2018, when I first lit a candle to read the runes of HTML by Bag-End’s hearth.

Since then I have wandered far: through the misty peaks of JavaScript, the deep mines of databases, and the golden halls of design. I make my home now in the fellowship of builders, shaping tools that are kind to the eye and faithful to the hand.

The arts I have lately practiced in my workshop:
